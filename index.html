Background

Built different,
from the start

I grew up in a classic Indian high school where entrepreneurship was not a subject — it was barely a concept. Public speaking was for the annual day. AI was science fiction.

So I built my own curriculum. I took initiative on things nobody else was doing — and learned what it means to ship something from nothing with zero institutional support.

At 15, I founded a clothing brand. Not a school project — a real brand that got recognition beyond the school gates. I learned customer discovery, Instagram growth, and what separates a product from a business.

Then I started helping other brands scale on Instagram. That's when the distribution obsession clicked: the problem wasn't the product, it was always the reach. Every founder I worked with had the same unsolved question — how do I get people to actually see this?

CREATR is my answer. The distribution infrastructure consumer AI apps don't have yet — and the reason I'm applying to Claude Corps.

I've used Claude as a cofounder, a strategist, a coder, and a thinking partner. I understand what it can do and more importantly what it can't yet. That gap is where I want to build.

I take initiative without a PM. I'm energized by ambiguity — every company I've built was started in a vacuum. And I can explain a technical system to a non-technical room because I've had to, from the day I first had to explain something that didn't exist yet to someone who didn't believe in it.
